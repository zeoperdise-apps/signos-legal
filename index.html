開発者は、これらのクラウドサービス提供事業者との間で、適切なデータ管理体制を維持するよう努めます。

Ranking Battles in the Game are asynchronous. As a result, the following player-created information is transmitted to the devices of other players who face you as an opponent, and may be viewed in-game. Please use this feature with that in mind.

  • Ranking information such as rating, win/loss record, and seasonal standing

The Game does not display your Steam persona name or avatar to other players in-game. The Game also has no free-form chat or text-entry feature. No information beyond the above is made visible to other players.

Note that the visibility of your Steam achievements and profile is governed by your own Steam profile settings and is controlled by Valve Corporation.

The Game's Ranking Battle feature stores and processes necessary data using cloud infrastructure (Amazon Web Services). Data is stored in the Asia Pacific (Tokyo) region, located in Japan.

If you reside in the EEA or the UK, your personal data may be processed in Japan. Japan has been recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of personal data protection, and such transfers are made on the basis of that adequacy decision (GDPR Art. 45). For any transfer to a country not covered by an adequacy decision, we implement appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

The Developer strives to maintain appropriate data management practices with this cloud service provider.

The Developer will not sell, rent, or disclose collected information to third parties without consent, except as required by law and in the following cases:

  • As described in Section 3, where the asynchronous battle feature displays certain information to other players who face you as an opponent

  • Where disclosure is required by a court or regulatory authority under applicable law
